## 3. The chain is public

Your wallets' addresses and all their transactions stay on the Solana network, visible to anyone, forever. No one,
PurpleSky included, can erase them.

## 4. Who they are shared with

- **Privy** (Privy, Inc., United States): creates your trading wallet and handles the sign-in.
- **Our Solana network access provider** (United States): it sees the transactions we send.
- **Our server provider** (United States): hosts our server.
- **GitHub** (United States): hosts this site and may log technical data about visits.

Your data is therefore transferred to the United States. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We
will hand it to an authority only when the law requires it.

## 5. How long

- Your periods' data, while you use the service and afterwards for as long as tax law requires.
- Waitlist data, until you ask us to delete it or, at the latest, 12 months after we open.
- The server's technical logs, 30 days.
